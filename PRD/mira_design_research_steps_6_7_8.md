# Mira V0.03 Design Research — Steps 6, 7 & 8: Unmoderated Interaction Testing, Visual Identity Systems & Real-World Constraint Protocol

> **Status:** This is a research plan, not completed user research or a product specification. Cohort details, device bands, app habits, visual directions, interaction concepts, exact timings, and pass counts below are hypotheses to test. The approved `mira_prd.md` and project guidance remain authoritative for V0.03 product logic and production behavior. Prototype preference alone does not change product scope.

## 0. Architectural & Workflow Continuity

Building on the three low-fidelity core loop concepts from **Step 5**, this document proposes **Steps 6, 7, and 8** of the Mira Design Research Plan (Lines 13–19):
1. **Step 6 (Test Interaction Model Before Visual Style):** An exploratory, zero-explanation usability test to learn how participants use the current Talk → Do flow and respond to alternative UI concepts. New concepts are prototypes only; keep V0.03 product logic and intervention rules fixed.
2. **Step 7 (Test Visual Directions on the Same UX):** Compare minimal/no central presence, subtle abstract presence, and restrained 3D/character presence on the same tested interaction layout. Do not assume an orb or visual direction wins.
3. **Step 8 (Test Real Constraints):** Explore hardware, lighting, acoustic, ergonomic, and network conditions as provisional test scenarios, not universal user facts or automatic engineering requirements.

## 1. Step 6: Test Interaction Model Before Visual Style (5–8 User Zero-Explanation Protocol)

### 1.1 Provisional Sample & Recruitment Hypotheses
Use **5–8 exploratory participants as a working range**, not a claim of representativeness or a guarantee of finding a particular percentage of usability issues. Set the final count and recruiting criteria before recruitment; the earlier draft used inconsistent 5–8 and 6–8 ranges.

The age, city, device price, app-use, and prior-therapy criteria below are recruitment hypotheses only. Do not treat them as a defined persona or exclude people solely to match unvalidated assumptions.

* **Provisional participant screener (all details below are hypotheses):**
  * **Age & Location:** 18–30 years old, living in Tier-1/Tier-2 urban India (Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi-NCR, Pune, Hyderabad).
  * **Living Context:** Include a mix of shared and private living spaces if feasible. The original "5 of 8" target is provisional, not a quota or audience fact.
  * **Device & App Habits:** Primary phone is a ₹12,000–₹25,000 Android device; uses [WhatsApp](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Ffaq.whatsapp.com%2F657157755756612) voice notes/calls and [PhonePe/Google Pay](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fthe-ken.com%2Fthe-collection%2Fsachin-bansal-loosens-the-phonepe-google-pay-grip-that-npcis-upi-cap-couldnt%2F) daily; zero prior usage of clinical CBT apps required.
* **Counterbalanced Prototype Order:** If concepts are distinct enough to compare, vary their order across participants. The `360×800 dp` device/layout is a test setup hypothesis; adapt to participants' actual devices and record differences.

### 1.2 Observe Before Prompting
The moderator **must not explain what buttons do, how Mira listens, or how to use the prototype**. First show the prototype without a situational prompt and observe what the participant does naturally. Only if a behavior relevant to the study does not occur on its own, offer a neutral scenario prompt and mark the behavior as prompted rather than unprompted. The scenario below is an optional follow-up, not the opening instruction:

> *"Imagine it's 11:30 PM in your room, your head is spinning with three things you haven't finished today, and you open this app. Show me what you would do—and do whatever feels natural."*

### 1.3 Seven Interaction Checkpoints (Current Baseline and Prototype Concepts)

During the exploratory test, the observer logs behavior without intervening at first. These checkpoints are prompts for what to observe, not mandatory tasks. The current V0.03 build is the baseline. Any checkpoint requiring a three-choice sheet, mute/VAD controls, mic-locked brain dump, or lock-screen timer must use a clearly labeled research prototype; those concepts are not current behavior or approved requirements. Exact timings and pass counts are provisional. Record actual behavior and calibrate measures after pilot sessions.

| Checkpoint | Interaction Being Tested | Moderator Trigger (If Not Reached Naturally) | Pass Criterion (Zero-Explanation Success) | Validates Assumption |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Start & Talk** (`Open → Start Call`) | Does the user recognize this is a live voice session and start speaking naturally? | None (observe first 10 seconds after launch). | User taps `Start Call` within **≤3 seconds** and speaks their first sentence without hunting for a chat box. | Core Call Mental Model |
| **2. Answer a Choice** (`Mira Asks → Choose`) | When Mira offers 3 options on the Temporary Choice Sheet, how does the user respond? | If user holds phone to ear, observe if the spatial chime prompts them to look at the screen. | User either **taps one of the 3 pills** OR **speaks the choice aloud** within **≤4 seconds** without asking *"Can I tap this?"* | `[Assumption A2]` (Ear-to-Hand + Dual Parity) |
| **3. Stop / Mute / Interrupt** | Does the user know how to silence their mic or stop Mira mid-sentence? | Moderator knocks on the table: *"Your mom just called out from the hall—pause or mute for a second."* | User locates and taps `Mute` in the Call Dock (or taps the canvas to pause) in **≤2 seconds** with one thumb. | Call Dock Ergonomics |
| **4. Switch to Text Mid-Call** (`Voice → Text Fallback`) | Can the user switch from speaking to typing when someone enters the room? | *"Someone just walked into your room and you can't speak out loud anymore, but you want to finish your thought."* | User taps the `[Keyboard]` icon in the dock, types a Hinglish/English reply in [Gboard](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fplay.google.com%2Fstore%2Fapps%2Fdetails%3Fid%3Dcom.google.android.inputmethod.latin%26hl%3Den_IN), and verifies Mira's loudspeaker voice automatically muted. | `[Assumption A1]` (Coupled Privacy Switch) |
| **5. Run Intervention A: Grounding (~60–90s)** | Does the user understand step pacing, remaining duration, and how to pause/exit early? | User selects `Take 60s to ground` from the Choice Sheet. | User follows the synced ring/prompts, identifies the segmented top progress bar ([Instagram Reels style](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fmetricool.com%2Fpause-instagram-reels%2F)), and successfully pauses/resumes once. | Guided Pacing Clarity |
| **6. Run Intervention B: Brain Dump** | Does the user understand that the mic is locked and Mira will **not** interrupt their pauses? | User selects `Brain dump everything` and is asked to pause for 5 seconds mid-thought. | User sees the `Mic Locked` waveform ([WhatsApp Voice Note grammar](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Ffaq.whatsapp.com%2F657157755756612)), pauses without anxiety, taps `Done Speaking`, and chooses whether to sort into 3 cards. | `[Assumption A3]` (Hands-Free Dump Lock) |
| **7. Run Intervention C: Tiny Next Step & Return** | Does the user understand choosing 1 physical action, locking the phone during the timer, and returning to Mira? | User selects `Pick one tiny next step` → `Drink a glass of water (02:00)`. | User locks the phone screen, sees the Android Live Update timer notification ([Google Clock style](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.androidauthority.com%2Fgoogle-clock-live-updates-rollout-3694162%2F)), unlocks, taps `I did it!`, and lands back in the active call. | `[Assumption A4]` & `[Assumption A5]` |

### 1.4 Decision Gate Before Step 7
Do not declare a winner from the proposed timing or count thresholds alone. Compare behavior patterns, record where participants hesitate or need prompting, and revise prototypes for another test if needed. No interaction architecture is preselected. Keep the current PRD's one-activity offer, explicit push-to-talk, consent, and intervention rules fixed in the V0.03 flow. Alternative concepts may be shown as research prototypes only; any product-logic change needs evidence and a separate scope decision.

## 2. Step 7: Test Visual Identities (3 Directions, Same UX)

Once the interaction model passes Step 6, we apply **three distinct visual identity systems** to the exact same locked UX layout (`360×800 dp` coordinates). The goal is to test with users: **What feels trustworthy, premium, private, and distinctly Mira?**

### 2.1 Side-by-Side Comparison of the 3 Visual Directions

| Visual Attribute | Direction 1: "Ultra-Clean / Minimal" (Precision Audio Utility) | Direction 2: "Warm / Tactile" (Dusk Paper & Terracotta Stone) | Direction 3: "Character / 3D Presence" (Living Organic Entity) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Core Vibe & Mental Model** | Feels like a flagship system call utility ([Phone by Google](https://support.google.com/phoneapp/answer/9118387?hl=en) meets [Stoic](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fapps.apple.com%2Fus%2Fapp%2Fstoic-journal-mental-health%2Fid1312926037)). | **Primary Background (Dark Default)** | `#111315` (OLED-safe Carbon Slate—avoids `#000000` black smearing on mid-range AMOLEDs per [Mobile Dark Mode Best Practices](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fphone-simulator.com%2Fblog%2Fdark-mode-implementation-best-practices-for-mobile-in-2026)) | `#1C1917` (Warm Roasted Espresso) with `#26221F` elevated tactile surface cards | `#0F141C` (Deep Midnight Indigo) with radial ambient glow behind the central presence entity |
| **Light / Sunlight Mode Surface** | `#F8FAFC` (Crisp Alabaster) with `#0F172A` ink text | `#F7F3EE` (Warm Unbleached Cotton) with `#1F1A17` charcoal ink | `#F2F5F8` (Soft Cloud Mist) with high-contrast `#101828` text |
| **Primary Accent & Active Voice Token** | `#10B981` (Signal Emerald) for `Listening`; `#E2E8F0` (Pure Silver) for `Speaking` | `#E07A5F` (Warm Terracotta) for primary actions; `#F4A261` (Soft Amber) for voice glow | `#6366F1` → `#2DD4BF` (Bioluminescent Indigo-to-Teal gradient shader) |
| **Typography (`English + Hinglish`)** | **Sans-only:** `Inter` or `Plus Jakarta Sans` (`600` semi-bold headers, `400` body, tabular numerals `fontVariant: ['tabular-nums']` for timers) | **Editorial Pair:** `Instrument Serif` or `Newsreader` (`24sp`) for Mira's synced voice lines + `Plus Jakarta Sans` (`16sp`) for action pills | **Rounded Geometric:** `DM Sans` or `Outfit` (`500` medium) with soft rounded letterforms matching the organic presence |

### 2.2 How Users Evaluate the 3 Visual Directions (The 5-Attribute Card Sort)
After completing the functional tasks in Step 6, show the participant the **Call Screen**, **Temporary Choice Sheet**, and **Grounding / Brain Dump Screen** in all three visual directions side-by-side. Ask them to rank the three directions on five specific questions:

1. **Over-the-Shoulder Privacy:** *"If you were sitting in your living room or on the Mumbai metro and someone glanced at your phone, which screen would you feel most comfortable having open?"*
2. **Trust & Emotional Safety:** *"Which one feels like it actually takes your problem seriously without feeling like a hospital or clinic?"*
3. **Consumer Polish:** *"Which one feels like a top-tier app you'd keep on your home screen alongside Spotify, WhatsApp, and Instagram?"*
4. **Late-Night Comfort:** *"At 1:00 AM in bed with the lights off, which screen feels easiest on your eyes?"*
5. **Distinctly Mira:** *"Which one feels like 'Mira'—neither a generic AI coding chatbot nor a childish pet game?"*

> **Open hypothesis for Step 7:** No direction or combination is expected to win. Compare the same flow with minimal/no central presence, subtle abstract presence, and restrained 3D/character presence. Use participant observations and feedback to decide what to explore further; do not treat preference alone as authorization to change V0.03 product behavior.

## 3. Step 8: Test Real Constraints (8-Condition Android Field Stress-Test Matrix)

If the team runs Step 8, use these as provisional physical and environmental test scenarios. They are not universal audience facts, mandatory mitigations, or automatic release gates. Test the approved V0.03 build where applicable; any scenario requiring new capabilities stays a research hypothesis unless separately approved:

| Constraint Scenario | Provisional Test Condition | Failure Mode to Observe | Mitigation Hypothesis (not a requirement) | Signal to Record (not a pass threshold) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Budget / Mid-Range Android Hardware** | Test on a physical **₹12,000–₹16,000 Android device** (6GB RAM, Snapdragon 680 / Helio G88 / Dimensity 6100+, `360×800 dp`, e.g., Redmi Note / Samsung M-series) with WhatsApp and Instagram running in background ([Counterpoint India Share](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.instagram.com%2Fp%2FDeb6JZnG7cZ%2F)). | UI thread frame drops (`<45 fps`) when bottom sheet opens while WebRTC audio is streaming, or Android OS killing the app during **Tiny Next Step** lock-screen timer. | Run all orb/sheet animations on the UI thread via [react-native-reanimated worklets](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fdocs.swmansion.com%2Freact-native-reanimated%2Fdocs%2Fguides%2Fperformance%2F) and [@shopify/react-native-skia](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fmargelo.com%2Fblog%2Fprofiling-skia-reanimated-low-end-android); bind an Android Foreground Service (`foregroundServiceType="microphone"`) during active sessions. | **≥55 fps** sheet transitions; **0 audio dropouts** during 5-minute session. |
| **2. Direct Outdoor Sunlight (`>10,000 Lux`)** | Test outdoors at 1:00 PM Indian afternoon sun on a mid-range screen (500–600 nits peak brightness) with finger smudges on the glass ([Android Screen Readability Guide](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwhizz-mobile.com%2Fguides%2Fandroid-phone-screen-hard-to-read%2F)). | Low-contrast muted gray captions or subtle translucent choice pills wash out completely and become unreadable. | Enforce **≥7:1 WCAG AAA contrast** on primary call controls and synced captions; provide automatic high-contrast light mode (`#F7F3EE` surface with `#1F1A17` text) or ultra-bright `#F8FAFC` text on dark slate. | 100% of choice pills and captions readable at arm's length in direct sun. |
| **3. Pitch-Black Bedroom (`0 Lux, 1 AM`)** | Test in a dark room with Android system brightness slider set to **5%–10%**, holding phone 10 inches from face on a pillow ([NN/g Dark Mode Issues](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.nngroup.com%2Farticles%2Fdark-mode-users-issues%2F)). | Pure white cards or bright modal sheets cause painful pupil constriction ("flashbang effect"); pure `#000000` backgrounds smear purple on cheap OLEDs when scrolling/dragging sheets. | Default to **Warm Dark Mode (`#161514` / `#1C1917`)**; cap maximum white text luminance at `#EDEDEF` (`90%` off-white); never flash a white background during state transitions. | Zero eye-squint when opening Choice Sheet or **Brain Dump** in a dark room. |
| **4. Headphones vs. No Headphones + Ear Proximity** | Start call with **no headphones** (held in hand → lifted to ear → lowered to hand for choice sheet), then plug in / connect Bluetooth TWS earbuds mid-call, then disconnect one earbud. | Proximity sensor fails to lock screen at ear (cheek hangs up call), or audio blasts out of loudspeaker when user expects earpiece privacy ([react-native-incall-manager routing](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fgithub.com%2Freact-native-webrtc%2Freact-native-incall-manager)). | Default no-headphone calls to **Earpiece** when proximity sensor is triggered, and show a prominent top pill (`Audio: Earpiece ▾` / `Speaker ▾`). If Bluetooth disconnects mid-call, **auto-mute TTS for 2 seconds** and fall back to Earpiece—never auto-blast loudspeaker. | **0 accidental loudspeaker leaks** on Bluetooth disconnect or ear-to-hand shift. |
| **5. Noisy Shared Environment (`65–70 dB`)** | Test under a high-speed ceiling fan + background Hindi/English TV or street traffic noise (65–70 dB SPL). | WebRTC VAD mistakes ceiling fan or background voices for user speech, preventing Mira from replying—or cuts the user off mid-sentence. | Surface a 1-tap **`[Noisy room? Tap to Hold/Lock Mic]`** chip whenever continuous background noise prevents clean VAD turn detection, and hard-disable VAD interruption in **Brain Dump**. | User can complete a full turn and a **Brain Dump** in a 70 dB room with 0 false cutoffs. |
| **6. Strict One-Handed Thumb Operation** | User holds phone in **one hand only** (tested separately with right hand and left hand) while standing or lying on their side, prohibited from using their second hand or shifting grip. | Top-corner close buttons (`y < 200dp`) or small pause icons require a dangerous grip shift on tall 6.6" (20:9) phones. | 100% of session-critical actions (`Start`, `Choice Pills`, `Pause`, `Done Dumping`, `I did it!`, `Mute`, `Keyboard`, `End`) sit inside **`y = 440–800 dp`** with **`≥48×48 dp`** hit targets ([Material Design 3 Touch Targets](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fm3.material.io%2Ffoundations%2Fdesigning%2Fstructure)). Also support Android system edge-back gesture to close temporary sheets. | Full 6-stage loop completable with one thumb without regripping the phone. |
| **7. Weak Internet & 5G↔4G Jitter** | Use Android Developer Options / network link conditioner to inject **350ms RTT latency, 8% packet loss, and a 3-second complete packet drop** mid-call ([Opensignal India Network Metrics](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.opensignal.com%2Four-approach%2Fmobile-metrics)). | User speaks, hears 4 seconds of dead silence, assumes the call died, says *"Hello? Hello?"*, and hangs up. | Render immediate client-side visual feedback: within `400ms` of user finishing speech, Orb shifts to `Thinking...`; if WebRTC RTT exceeds `800ms` or ICE disconnects, show `Hold on, weak signal—reconnecting...` with a soft comforting pulse while keeping local **Brain Dump** / choice taps queued. | 0 lost user inputs during a 3-second packet drop; clear visual status within `500ms`. |
| **8. Instant Text Fallback (`Shared-Room Intrusion`)** | Halfway through Mira speaking aloud on speakerphone, user taps `[Keyboard]` because a parent/roommate walked in. | Mira keeps talking out loud while the keyboard opens, exposing the user's private conversation to the room. | **Coupled Privacy Switch (`[Assumption A1]`):** Tapping `[Keyboard]` when no headphones are connected **immediately fades out Mira's TTS audio within `150ms`**, switches output state to `Muted (Reading Mode)`, and streams the rest of Mira's response as synced 2-line text above [Gboard](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fplay.google.com%2Fstore%2Fapps%2Fdetails%3Fid%3Dcom.google.android.inputmethod.latin%26hl%3Den_IN). | Loudspeaker audio silences in **`<150ms`** upon tapping `[Keyboard]`. |
